# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Patients in Germany, many of them international** (Anna, 32, public insurance, English and Russian; Omar, 41, English and Arabic). On a phone, often in a hurry or unwell, sometimes in a second language. Job: find a doctor who speaks their language and accepts their insurance, and book the earliest real appointment.
- **Doctors** (Dr. Emre Aydın, GP). On a phone between patients. Job: know who is next and why before the door opens; handle delays.
- **Practice staff** (Sabine, practice manager / MFA at reception). At a desktop, all day, phone ringing. Job: keep the calendar full and correct, confirm requests, resolve conflicts, fill cancelled slots.

## Product Purpose

Parlo is a three-sided doctor booking platform for Germany: the right doctor, in your language, at the earliest real slot; for practices, no empty chair when someone cancels. The prototype exists for a portfolio case study (screenshots, live demo, Storybook). Success: the hero scenario (Anna cancels → Omar takes the slot from the waitlist → reception, doctor and insights update) works by clicking across all three roles.

## Brand Personality

Calm, human, precise. Warm enough to welcome someone new in the country, clear enough for a busy reception desk. Plain words, sentence case, formal "Sie" in German. Leads with time and language. Never promises medical outcomes.

## Anti-references

- The default "medical blue" health app, stock photos of smiling doctors, clinical white-and-blue sterility.
- Flags for languages (flags are countries, not languages).
- Marketplace cards that lead with star ratings instead of availability.
- Dashboards that are a grid of links or vanity metrics instead of telling staff what to do now.
- Symptom-checker language and anything that sounds like medical advice.

## Design Principles

1. Earliest real slot first: time beats ratings.
2. Language is visible everywhere, as codes and names.
3. Tell staff what to do now.
4. One schedule, three views: a change is instantly true for patient, doctor and reception.
5. Consent is explicit and reversible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA in light and dark. Status always icon plus text. 44 pt touch targets on mobile. Reduced motion respected. German copy must fit without overflow. Users may read in a second language: short sentences, no idioms.
