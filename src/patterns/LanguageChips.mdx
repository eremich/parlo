## Why codes, not flags

A flag is a country, not a language. Arabic is spoken in more than twenty countries; Switzerland has four
official languages; a Ukrainian flag would exclude Ukrainians who prefer Russian. Codes are neutral, short enough
for a result card, and screen readers hear the full names ("Speaks English, Russian, Ukrainian, German").

## Accessibility

- The list has one accessible name with full language names; the visual codes are hidden from screen readers.
- A match is shown with a check icon as well as color.
