# Doctor result card

<Lead>
  One result in the patient's search. It answers the questions in the order a patient asks them: when, who, where,
  in which language, with which insurance.
</Lead>

## Anatomy

| Order | Element | Why here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Earliest slot, distance | Time beats ratings. The first thing you read is when you can go. |
| 2 | Doctor, specialty, practice | Who and where. The whole card opens the profile. |
| 3 | Language chips, insurance, visit type | Can I talk to them, will my insurance cover it. The patient's languages are highlighted. |
| 4 | Rating | Present but last; rating alone never ranks a result. |
| 5 | Three bookable times | Book without opening the profile. "All times" opens the calendar. |

Kept from the 2019 original: times right in the results. Changed: the earliest slot moved from the bottom to the
top, and languages became a first-class attribute.

## Specialists

<Canvas of={Stories.Specialist} />

Specialists often have their earliest slot weeks away and expect a referral (Überweisung). The card says so up
front, so nobody learns it at the reception desk.

## German

<Canvas of={Stories.German} />

German labels are longer; the chip row wraps and the rating moves to its own line when needed.

## Accessibility

- One stretched button (the doctor's name) makes the card clickable; slot buttons sit above it and have their own
  labels ("Book 08:40").
- Distance and rating are text, not icons alone.
