## Personality

Calm, human, precise. A deep green-teal instead of the default medical blue, warm enough for someone new in the
country and clear enough for a busy reception desk.

## Platforms

| Product | Frame | Conventions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patient app | 390 × 844 | Apple HIG: large titles, labeled tab bar, sheets with detents, 44 pt targets |
| Doctor app | 390 × 844 | Same as patient; "My day" first |
| Practice dashboard | 1440 × 900 | Left navigation with labels, content max width, keyboard shortcuts in the calendar |

## Source of truth

Code is the source of truth; this Storybook renders it. Tokens live in `src/styles/tokens.css` and are bridged to
Tailwind in `src/styles/index.css`. The Foundations pages parse those files, so they cannot drift.
